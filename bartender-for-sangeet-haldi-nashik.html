Frequently Asked Questions

Can you do a fire flair show at a Sangeet?

Yes, provided the venue allows it and there is adequate outdoor space for safety.

What drinks are popular for Haldi ceremonies?

Yellow and orange-themed mocktails, mango infusions, and refreshing citrus coolers are highly requested.

Do you serve shots on the dance floor?

Yes, we can arrange for tray service to bring shooters directly to the dance floor.

How long does the service last for a Sangeet?

Our standard packages cover 4 to 5 hours of active pouring, with options to extend.

Can you coordinate with the DJ?

Definitely. We often time our flair shows and special pours with the music.
